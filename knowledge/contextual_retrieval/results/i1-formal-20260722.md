## Conclusion

The complete 450-case MultiHop-RAG retrieval-only A/B produced valid I1
evidence, but the registered I1 method gate failed. Contextual B did not improve
either Recall@10 gate metric, and the inference subset regressed.

This closes only the sealed retrieval-only I1 hypothesis. It does not decide
the separate Agentic I2 and does not establish a general framework-adoption or
rejection policy. No Agent, RAGAS evaluator, or Judge was initialized.

The generated raw data, Context cache, database, per-request samples, and
service logs are intentionally not committed. This report preserves only
aggregate, sanitized evidence.

## Decision

identity.

must be interpreted using its own end-to-end evidence, not as a continuation
